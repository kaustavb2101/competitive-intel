1Q26 Key Takeaway and Outlook
Loan Growth and Customer Expansion
• Temporary tightening in flood-affected areas since Dec-25; expected to resume in 2Q26, with customer
acquisition continuing to outpace portfolio growth
• Continued focus on risk-adjusted returns and disciplined underwriting
• Truck portfolio saw softer demand, overall portfolio quality remains stable with no significant deterioration
• YFWH to step up to 70% by 2Q26
Asset Quality: Strong reserves and clean balance sheet
• No material impact observed in 1Q26; credit cost supported by prior overlays and support measures, with
ongoing monitoring of early indicators and external uncertainties
• Collections manageable with proactive management and mitigation programs
• LOS improved YoY; used car prices expected to remain stable into 2Q26
Insurance brokerage: Market-Leading Growth Continues
• No impact on volume or product mix; continued market share gains
• Non-motor insurance remains an additional growth opportunity, with further room to scale
• Diversified platform and self-service adoption continue to enhance productivity and fee income
Funding & Liquidity
• Prudent leverage and strong credit profile support market access, with ample liquidity
• Full-year 2026 funding cost is expected to improv, compared to 2025
Cost & Efficiency
• Some pressure from external factors (e.g., oil and electricity), well managed
• Continued investment in technology, AI, and workforce to enhance productivity
Capital Allocation
• Maintain flexibility across growth, dividends, and buybacks, focused on long-term value creation
