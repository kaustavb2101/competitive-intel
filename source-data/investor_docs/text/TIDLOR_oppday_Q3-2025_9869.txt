3Q25 Takeaways and Current Outlook: Driving in a Fog
Loan Growth & Customer Expansion
- Early signs of Q4 acceleration due to loan campaign
- 2025 loan growth expected to in line with 2024, with continued focus on risk-adjusted returns and
customer acquisition which continue to outpace loan portfolio growth
- “You Fight, We Help” (YFWH) Program <2% of portfolio enrolled
High-Quality Portfolio & Prudent Risk Management
- NPL ratio expected to stay flat and below 2%
- Credit cost and NPL formation projected to be below 3%
- Loss on sale improves YoY, with used car prices expected to soften by Q1/26
Insurance Brokerage: Market-Leading Growth
- Non-life premium growth for 2025 projected in low-double digits, outpacing loan growth
- Diversified brokerage platform and strong digital self-service adoption continue to drive fee income
growth and productivity improvement
Efficient Capital & Funding Management
- Well-diversified funding structure with ample liquidity and prudent leverage to support sustainable growth
Cost Efficiency & Investment in Growth
- Cost base elevated to support 4Q ramp-up and 2026 expansion
- Ongoing investment in workforce development, tech platforms enhances productivity, customer
experience, and marketing efficiency
Restructuring plan update: Insurtech delayed due to bandwidth consumed by recent shareholder changes.
Likely to resume next year.
