HENG Strategy 2025
"In 2025, HENG is committed to delivering Sustainable Growth through rigorous
credit screening, effective cost and asset quality control, and the strategic
expansion of business opportunities via a robust partner network."
Quality Portfolio Management
Quality over Quantity: Shift lending policy to prioritize "Quality" over volume growth
(Selective Growth) to build a resilient long-term portfolio.
Tech-Driven Screening: Enhance customer screening processes using Credit Scoring
and Data Analytics for faster and more accurate risk assessment.
Targeted Segments: Focus on high-potential customer segments with good
repayment history, specifically prioritizing Secured Loans.
