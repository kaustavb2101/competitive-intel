1. Total revenue for the 6-month ended June 30, 2026 was 1,686.0 million baht compared to total
revenue of 1,685.2 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It increased by 0.7 million
baht, representing a 0.04% increase. This was mainly due to flat loan growth in the first half of
2026. In this regard, the Company has already opened 35 new branches in the second quarter of
2026.
2. Total expenses for the 6-month ended June 30, 2026 was 1,114.2 million baht compared to total
expenses of 1,126.6 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 12.4
million baht, representing a 1.1% decrease. This was mainly due to decreased of expected credit
loss compared to the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 47.5 million baht, and
decreasing interest rate from financial institutions, resulting in decreased of finance costs compared
to the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 27.9 million baht.

1. Total assets: The Company had total assets as of June 30, 2026 of 16,264.9 million baht, an
increase of 621.6 million baht from the year 2025, representing a 4.0% increase. This was mainly
due to growth of loan portfolio increased amount 748.1 million baht from the year 2025.
2. Total liabilities: The Company had total liabilities as of June 30, 2026 of 9,381.1 million baht,
an increase of 585.5 million baht from the year 2025, representing a 6.7% increase. This was
mainly due to management of borrowings from financial institutions to be in line with the
Company’s loan portfolio.
3. Equity: The Company had equity as of June 30, 2026 of 6,883.8 million baht, an increase of 36.1
million baht from the year 2025, representing a 0.5% increase. The Company’s equity increased
from net profit for the 6-month of 454.2 million baht. On 22 April 2026, the Annual General
Meeting of the Company’s shareholders for the year 2026, approved the resolution regarding to
the payment of dividend from net profit for the year ended 31 December 2025 at the rate of
Baht 0.20 per share, amounting to 419.2 million baht. The dividend was paid on 20 May 2026.
4. D/E Ratio: The Company had a debt to equity ratio (D/E ratio) as of June 30, 2026 of 1.36 and
as at December 31, 2025 of 1.28. The increment of D/E ratio was because total liabilities has
increased to be higher than the equity.
