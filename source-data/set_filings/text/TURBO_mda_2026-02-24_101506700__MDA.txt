Ngernturbo PCL Announces 2025 Annual Financial Results
For the year 2025, the Company reported a Net Profit of THB 503.1 million, representing a growth of
255.3% compared to 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, Net Profit stood at THB 141.5 million, an increase
of 12.6% (QoQ).
This growth was mainly driven by the loan portfolio expansion and growth in the insurance brokerage
business compared to the third quarter of 2025. Concurrently, the Company remains committed to its policy of
AI technology integration, reflected in reduced administrative expenses compared to 2024, and the ongoing
rationalization of overlapping branch operations. Furthermore, the Company has continued to improve its
rigorous credit approval and debt collection processes, with the NPL ratio (Stage 3) improving from 4.18% to
3.96% between Q3 and Q4 2025.
Overview of Operating Results for Q4 2025
Quarter-on-Quarter Comparison (QoQ)
In Q4/2025, the Company recorded a net profit of THB 141.5 million, a growth of 12.6% from the
previous quarter (QoQ), primarily due to the following factors:
1. Total Income amounted to THB 814.2 million , an increase of THB 51.3 million or 6.7% (QoQ). This
resulted from the Company’s loan portfolio expansion from THB 10,714.8 million to THB 11,102.5
million while maintaining a loan yield of approximately 24.0%. The insurance brokerage business also
grew significantly due to increased product diversity and a higher number of insurance partners.
2. Administrative Expenses increased by THB 27.9 million (QoQ) , driven by seasonally higher
branch renovation costs and increased personnel-related expenses, which is in line with company’s
performance.
3. Expected Credit Loss (ECL) increased by THB 12.9 million (QoQ) due to the consideration of
management overlays of 5.0% of the total allowance for doubtful accounts, resulting in an increase in
allowance for doubtful accounts of approximately THB 21.9 million. While this overlay was the primary
factor behind the rise in the Credit Cost ratio, excluding this item would show a declining Credit Cost
trend. This aligns with improved asset quality and a reduction in the Stage 3 ratio, which decreased
from 4.18% in Q3 2025 to 3.96% in Q4 2025. The Management Overlay was implemented under the
principle of Prudence as a precautionary measure to mitigate economic uncertainties and to prepare
for the standards of Development Banks in the future.
4. Finance Costs decreased by THB 10.2 million (QoQ) due to changes in the capital structure
following the listing on the SET, coupled with a decrease in the Company's borrowing rates, which
aligned with the direction of the policy interest rate.
Year-on-Year Comparison (YoY)
In Q4/2025, the Company recorded a net profit of THB 141.5 million, a growth of 163.5% from the
previous year (YoY), primarily due to the following factors:
1. Total Income amounted to THB 814.2 million, an increase of THB 21.3 million (YoY), driven by
significant growth in the insurance brokerage business resulting from increased product diversity and
a higher number of insurance partners.
2. Administrative Expenses decreased by THB 8.2 million (YoY) due to efficient branch
management, the optimization of overlapping branches, and the organization integration of
technology.
3. Expected Credit Loss (ECL) decreased by THB 45.2 million (YoY) due to improved asset quality
achieved through rigorous credit approval and debt collection processes, as well as an increase in
bad debt recovery.
4. Finance Costs decreased by THB 28.6 million (YoY) due to changes in the capital structure
following the listing on the Stock Exchange. Additionally, the Company's borrowing rates decreased in
line with the policy interest rate.

Overview of 2025 Operating Results
For the full year of 2025, Total Income was THB 3,094.7 million, a growth of 2.0% (YoY). Net Profit for
2025 stood at THB 503.1 million , an increase of 255.3% (YoY), driven primarily by the following factors:
1. Effective Loan Portfolio Management: Interest income continued to grow robustly, even as the
overall loan portfolio slightly decreased.
2. Efficient Cost Management: Administrative expenses decreased by 10.6% (YoY) to THB 1,475.6
million due to the optimization of overlapping branches and the organization-wide integration of AI
technology.
3. Credit Risk Management: Expected Credit Loss (ECL) decreased by 21.3% (YoY) to THB 600.2
million, reflecting an improvement in asset quality resulting from enhanced risk management
procedures in both lending and debt collection, as well as an increase in bad debt recovery.
4. Reduced Finance Costs: Finance costs decreased by 10.9% (YoY) as a result of lower interest rates
and reduced borrowing from financial institutions, consistent with the cautious lending approach
adopted in 2025.

Financial Position Overview
Quarter-on-Quarter Comparison (QoQ)
● Assets: As of December 31, 2025, the Company had total assets of THB 12,677.2 million, a slight
decrease of 2.4% from the previous quarter (QoQ). The primary cause was efficient liquidity
management; cash and cash equivalents decreased by THB 664.9 million, which was utilized to
support loan portfolio expansion.
● Liabilities: The Company’s total liabilities decreased by 4.8% (QoQ), primarily driven by repayments
of loans from financial institutions, resulting in an overall reduction in debt burden and finance costs.
● Shareholders' Equity: Shareholders' equity increased by 3.9% (QoQ), directly resulting from growth
in operating performance, which led to an increase in retained earnings
Year-on-Year Comparison (YoY)
● Assets: As of December 31, 2025, the Company had total assets of THB 12,677.2 million, a slight
decrease of 1.1% from the end of 2024. The implementation of a rigorous lending policy consistent
with economic conditions resulted in a 0.3% decrease in net loans and hire purchase receivables
compared to the end of 2024.
● Liabilities: Total liabilities decreased significantly by 12.8% from the end of 2024. The main cause
was the management of excess liquidity to repay loans from financial institutions, consistent with the
reduced size of the loan portfolio, thereby helping to reduce interest expenses.
● Shareholders' Equity: Shareholders' equity increased by 44.7% from the end of 2024. The key factor
was the capital raising through the Initial Public Offering (IPO) of ordinary shares to the general public,
as well as increased retained earnings from continued operational growth.
