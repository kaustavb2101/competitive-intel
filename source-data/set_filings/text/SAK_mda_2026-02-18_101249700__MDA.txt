1. Total revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025 was 3,340.9 million baht compared to total
revenue of 3,123.5 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It increased by 217.4
million baht, representing a 7.0% increase. This was mainly due to increase from new customer
base by expansion business plan of 50 new branches in year 2025.
2. Total expenses for the year ended December 31, 2025 was 2,237.8 million baht compared to total
expenses of 2,074.3 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It increased by 163.5
million baht, representing a 7.9% increase. This was mainly due to opening new branches in year
2025, and increased in the number of employee, resulting to the Company’s selling and
administrative expenses of 1,594.2 million baht, compared to selling and administrative expenses
of 1,466.6 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It increased by 127.6 million baht,
representing a 8.7% increase. Expected credit loss compared to the same period of the previous
year. It increased by 24.9 million baht, which is in line with the Company’s loan portfolio.

1. Total assets: The Company had total assets as of December 31, 2025 of 15,643.3 million baht,
an increase of 327.2 million baht from the year 2024, representing a 2.1% increase. This was
mainly due to debt collection. Furthermore, resulting in increased of cash and cash equivalents
from the year 2024. It increased by 406.0 million baht.
2. Total liabilities: The Company had total liabilities as of December 31, 2025 of 8,795.6 million
baht, an increase of 173.4 million baht from the year 2024, representing a 1.9% increase. This
was mainly due to management of borrowings from financial institutions to be in line with the
Company’s loan portfolio.
3. Equity: The Company had equity as of December 31, 2025 of 6,847.7 million baht, an increase
of 500.7 million baht from the year 2024, representing a 7.9% increase. The Company’s equity
increased from net profit for the year of 874.3 million baht. On 22 April 2025, the Annual General
Meeting of the Company’s shareholders for the year 2025, approved the resolution regarding to
the payment of dividend from net profit for the year ended 31 December 2024 at the rate of
Baht 0.18 per share, amounting to 377.3 million baht. The dividend was paid on 20 May 2025.
4. D/E Ratio: The Company had a debt to equity ratio (D/E ratio) as of December 31, 2025 of 1.28
and as at December 31, 2024 of 1.41. The reduction of D/E ratio was because the equity has
increased to be higher than total liabilities.
