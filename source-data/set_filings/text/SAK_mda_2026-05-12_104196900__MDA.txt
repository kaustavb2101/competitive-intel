1. Total revenue for the 3-month ended March 31, 2026 was 803.1 million baht compared to total
revenue of 825.7 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 22.6 million
baht, representing a 2.7% decrease. This was mainly due to flat loan growth in the first quarter of
2026. In this regard, the Company plans to expand business by opening 35 new branches in the
second quarter of 2026.
2. Total expenses for the 3-month ended March 31, 2026 was 513.8 million baht compared to total
expenses of 540.1 million baht in the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 26.3 million
baht, representing a 4.9% decrease. This was mainly due to decreased of expected credit loss
compared to the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 30.3 million baht, and
decreasing interest rate from financial institutions, resulting in decreased of finance costs compared
to the same period of the previous year. It decreased by 14.0 million baht.

1. Total assets: The Company had total assets as of March 31, 2026 of 15,882.2 million baht, an
increase of 238.9 million baht from the year 2025, representing a 1.5% increase. This was mainly
due to growth of loan portfolio increased amount 135.9 million baht from the year 2025.
Furthermore, The Company reserved working capital to support loan expansion, resulting in
increased of cash and cash equivalents from the year 2025. It increased by 75.7 million baht.
2. Total liabilities: The Company had total liabilities as of March 31, 2026 of 8,782.6 million baht,
an decrease of 13.0 million baht from the year 2025, representing a 0.1% decrease. This was
mainly due to management of borrowings from financial institutions to be in line with the
Company’s loan portfolio.
3. Equity: The Company had equity as of March 31, 2026 of 7,099.6 million baht, an increase of
251.9 million baht from the year 2025, representing a 3.7% increase. The Company’s equity
increased by 229.4 million baht from consolidated net profit for the first quarter of 2026.
4. D/E Ratio: The Company had a debt to equity ratio (D/E ratio) as of March 31, 2026 of 1.24 and
as at December 31, 2025 of 1.28. The reduction of D/E ratio was because the equity has increased
to be higher than total liabilities.
