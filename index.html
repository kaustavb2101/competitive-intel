Business Loan White Space

Only MTC offers explicit nano/SME finance. TIDLOR & Ngern Hai Jai target specialized niches. เงินไชโย can position as the ”SME vehicle-backed loan” specialist with faster approval than banks and lower rates than MFI.

Land Deed Loans: Untapped Premium Margin

TIDLOR fixes land deed at 15% — the only competitor with a published rate. MTC charges 15–22%. A differentiated "fast chanote" product (3-day approval guarantee) at 15–18% with high LTV could win commercial borrowers.

MTC's 7,000-Branch Density Is Unbeatable Short-Term

MTC's distribution is its moat — not its rates (15–28%). เงินไชโย should avoid head-on geographic overlap with MTC in its strongholds (Central/Northeast). Instead, focus on secondary cities where MTC has ≤3 branches.
