## 2026-06-30 — Entry 1: Regulation, competitors, macro, datasets

### A. Regulation — BoT now directly supervises auto hire-purchase/leasing; rate caps tightening

- **FINDING:** A Royal Decree signed **5 Jun 2025** brings auto hire-purchase and leasing of cars & motorcycles under the Financial Institutions Business Act (FIBA) and direct Bank of Thailand supervision, **effective 2 Dec 2025**. BoT gains power to cap/monitor rates, fees and down payments, mandate approved contract structures, regulate repossession, and require licensing. (Conf: High)
  - https://www.nishimura.com/en/knowledge/publications/20250701-113806
  - https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3048236/bank-of-thailand-to-supervise-loans-for-hire-purchase-leasing
- **FINDING:** Existing rate ceilings: new-car HP ≤10%/yr, used-car ≤15%/yr, motorcycle ≤23%/yr; auto **title-loan** operators under a personal-loan licence need ≥฿50m registered capital and are capped at **28%** interest. Responsible-lending framework (BoT notification 25680030) is in force. Compliance timeline: notifications/hearings Sep–Nov 2025; **registration window closes Q1 2026**, non-compliant firms risk forced exit. (Conf: High)
  - https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2568/EngPDF/25680030.pdf
  - https://netsoltech.com/blog/decoding-the-bot-mandate
- **INTERPRETATION (Obj #1 & #2):** The 28% title-loan cap and responsible-lending rules compress the high-rate tail of the book and may *reduce credit access for marginal borrowers* — a headwind to both yield and volume growth toward 3,000 branches. The bigger strategic read: tighter supervision favors scaled, compliant incumbents and squeezes small/informal lenders out (forced exit by Q1 2026) — **white-space could open as sub-scale competitors retreat**, but our own underwriting/pricing must already assume a ≤28% world. Worth a simulator lever: yield-cap sensitivity at 28%.

### C. Macro — household debt, farm income, drought

- **FINDING:** Thailand household debt = **~86.7% of GDP (Dec 2025)**; **87.2% in Q3 2025** (up from 87.1% Q2 2025). Elevated debt is flagged as a key constraint on 2026 bank/credit growth. (Conf: Med — Trading Economics / CEIC summaries)
  - https://tradingeconomics.com/thailand/households-debt-to-gdp
  - https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3170443/household-debt-to-limit-bank-growth
- **FINDING:** Banking-system gross NPL ratio was **2.84%** (Q2 2024 reference, ฿540.8bn, driven by consumer loans); NPLs/SMs eased slightly in Q2 2025 but remain elevated. (Conf: Med — BoT quarterly brief)
  - https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20240827.html
- **FINDING — farm income / commodities (OAE):** 2026 agricultural GDP projected **+2.0–3.0%** (฿723.2–730.3bn, highest in 3 yrs). OAE 2026 price outlook: **firmer** prices for chicken, palm, cassava, durian; **softer** for rice (global oversupply, India resumed white-rice exports) and rubber. 2025/26 paddy output est. **35.44m tonnes** (main crop −0.32%, off-season −1.63%). (Conf: Med — Nation Thailand summarizing OAE)
  - https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40060299
  - https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40065283
- **FINDING — drought:** El Niño shift expected from **mid-2026, >80% probability**, peaking year-end; **May–Aug 2026 water shortages** likely outside irrigation zones; 2026 rainfall expected near 2012 (1,479mm vs 30-yr avg 1,500mm). (Conf: Med — Nation Thailand / BoT MPR box)
  - https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40066149
  - https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/documents/en/our-roles/monetary-policy/mpc-publication/monetary-policy-report/mpr-box/MPR_2023_Q3_BOX2.pdf
- **INTERPRETATION (Obj #1):** Diverging commodity outlook is directly actionable for the crop-stress engine. **Rice and rubber farmers face price pressure into 2026 AND drought from mid-year — double stress** → flag rice-heavy and rubber-heavy provinces as rising-PD in the agri/collateral lenses. Conversely, **cassava/palm/durian/chicken districts have improving cash flow** → safer to expand agri lending there. This maps cleanly onto build_crop_stress.py's price-stress + drought components; current global price proxy should be refreshed toward this 2026 OAE direction.
