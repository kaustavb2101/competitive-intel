Macro: the external conditions moving the book.

This is the Macro tab — external data only. Nothing here is our loan book; the book has its own tab. The question this answers is narrower and more useful: what is happening outside the company that will show up inside it.

Is the economy the problem?
No. Growth and inflation both came in above what the IMF projected for 2026, and rates are at 1.0%. The macro backdrop is a mild tailwind.
Are crop prices the problem?
No — they are a tailwind too. Rice +17.9%, rubber +32.4%, palm +18.2% YoY. Only sugar is falling hard (−13.5%).
So what is deteriorating?
The collateral. Used pickup values sit 50% below their peak and 33 points below their own 2015 base, and new pickup registrations have fallen 59% in three years. That is the pool we lend against, shrinking and cheapening at the same time.
And the borrower?
Drought, not price. 338 of 928 districts (36.4%) are in drought. The farm-price tailwind does not reach a household whose field did not yield.

Every figure carries its provenance. measured means a source published it. estimated means we modelled it. Read them differently.

Thailand has already overtaken the IMF's 2026 projection

The IMF projected 1.5% growth and 0.9% inflation for 2026. The measured outturns are +2.8% and +2.79% — 1.3 points and 1.9 points higher. Where a Thai measurement exists, we show it instead of the projection and say which is which.

One number is deliberately not on this page

The current account printed −7,591 USD million in April 2026 — but the trailing twelve months of that same series net to roughly +847M. A single month shown alone would read as a national crisis and would be wrong. The honest trailing figure is not yet built, so the chip waits.

Read the Beef row carefully — it is the trap on this board

The world beef index is +11.8%. Thai cattle prices are down about 40% from their 2022 peak. A cattle household in Isan is not experiencing +11.8%. The world index is the wrong instrument for that borrower, and the row says so rather than letting the green number stand.

The pickup–car gap is recent, not structural

Through most of 2013–2021 pickups held their value as well as or better than cars. The 20–34 point gap only opened from 2022 onward. Whatever is depressing pickup resale started four years ago and has not reversed — it is not a long-run property of the asset class.

Read 0.94% unemployment together with 63.2% informality, never instead of it

Headline unemployment is structurally near zero in Thailand because informal work absorbs the slack. A borrower who loses formal work does not appear in the unemployment number — they appear in the informal count, on a lower and less predictable income. The unemployment rate is not a stress signal for this book. Informality and the seasonal-idle share are.

Electrification is not yet a collateral problem — but it is the clock

BEVs are 0.95% of the fleet and diesel is still 56.3%. Nothing to act on this quarter. It is worth watching precisely because our collateral has a five-to-ten-year resale tail: the fleet mix that matters for recoveries in 2032 is being registered now.

Two different things, deliberately kept apart

The live pulse says what is happening this week. The structural census says which ground floods repeatedly, whatever the weather is doing today. The second is a hazard flag — did the ground flood, how often — and explicitly not a flooded-area or loss estimate, because the source's per-event polygons overlap and any area total from them would be wrong.

07What this tab cannot tell you

Four gaps, said plainly.

Open

  • The current account is a single month, not a trend. April 2026 shows −7,591 USD million against a trailing-twelve-month figure of roughly +847M. The trailing aggregate is not built yet, so the chip is withheld rather than shown misleadingly.
  • Government debt has no Thai source yet. It still reads from the IMF at 2025 vintage while every other chip on the strip is Thai-official and current.
  • The ฿44bn co-pay figure is unconfirmed. The likely referent is ไทยช่วยไทย พลัส (cabinet-approved 2026-05-19, government portion ≈฿49.6bn at its two-month mark) but it does not cleanly match ฿44bn, so it has been left rather than guessed at.
  • Drought is modelled, not observed. SPEI is an index derived from rainfall and evapotranspiration. It is the best national-coverage signal available, but no one has walked those 338 districts.

These are on the record because a number whose weakness is stated is worth more in a committee room than one that is quietly wrong.

08So what

Three things the macro picture asks of us.

Collateral valuation
Pickup resale is 33 points below its own 2015 base and the gap to cars opened only in 2022. If our advance rates on pickup titles were set against pre-2022 behaviour, they are set against a market that no longer exists.
Collateral supply
New pickup inflow is down 59% in three years while cars and motorcycles are flat. The pool we will be lending against in 2030 is being determined now, and it is shrinking faster than the fleet is.
Borrower stress
Watch drought and informality, not crop prices and not the unemployment rate. Prices are a tailwind; 36.4% of districts are dry; 63.2% of employment is informal; 60% of households cannot cover three months.

All three are readings of the network and book we already run. Nothing on this tab is an argument to open, close, or expand anything.
