Road to 3,000 · where the next ~1,000 branches go

AutoX has 2,015 branches today and targets 3,000 by the IPO window (~2027). This splits the 985 net-new branches across the 5 regions proportional to remaining headroom. Capacity proxy = regional workforce (informal + formal employment, NSO, measured) — a market-size stand-in, not a demand or revenue model. Saturation = branches per 100k workforce. Headroom = the gap to the branches-per-100k density that 3,000 branches implies nationally. The allocation is an illustrative planning split, not a committed branch plan — confirm with local site surveys.

Computing the road to 3,000…
Method & caveats

For each region: fair_share@3000 = 3,000 × (region workforce ÷ national workforce) · headroom = max(0, fair_share@3000 − current branches) · net-new allocated = (985 net-new) × (region headroom ÷ Σ headroom), rounded so targets sum to exactly 3,000 (largest-remainder). In plain terms: bring every region up to the same branches-per-100k-workforce density that 3,000 branches implies nationally; regions furthest below that line get the most new branches. Workforce is measured (NSO); the split is an illustrative proxy allocation, not a demand model.

Sequenced district plan · which branch to open next ESTIMATED

The regional split above says how many; this says where, in what order. All 985 net-new branches are placed one at a time, each going to the district with the highest remaining demand per outlet — the same divisor method used to allocate parliamentary seats (D'Hondt), so diminishing returns are built in. Placements within 15 km of each other cannibalize: a new branch also shrinks the value of its neighbors' next branch. Demand is risk-adjusted (a high district risk-proxy trims its pull by up to 25%). Click a row to see the district on the national map.

Method & caveats

Marginal value of the next branch in district i: v = D / (1 + e) where D = demand × (1 − 0.25 × risk_proxy/100) (demand: OSM footfall + DIW workers + vehicles, measured-derived; risk-proxy: estimated) and e = own + placed + 0.35 × Σ distance-weighted neighbor outlets within 15 km. Greedy placement of all net-new branches; ≤8 per district. Without the neighbor term this greedy is the provably optimal allocation of total demand-per-outlet (classic divisor-method result). The sequence is an ESTIMATED planning order, not a committed plan — every site needs a local survey. Built by pipeline/build_expansion_plan.py (deterministic, offline).

Competitor-exit white-space · regulatory tailwind

Regulatory tailwind (Q1 2026): under the BoT responsible-lending framework (notification 25680030) auto title-loan operators need a personal-loan licence (≥฿50m capital, 28% interest cap). The registration window closes Q1 2026 — non-compliant, sub-scale / informal lenders risk forced exit. Tighter supervision favours scaled compliant incumbents (MTC, SAWAD, Tidlor, Heng — and AutoX) and squeezes small operators out, so white-space may open as sub-scale rivals retreat. Source: docs/RESEARCH_DIGEST.md §A (Conf: High) · BoT 25680030.

Honest caveat — this is an ESTIMATED PROXY, not a measurement. The operators that will exit are exactly the sub-scale / informal lenders we do not census. Our competitor census covers only the four big, compliant brands (Heng · Muangthai · Tidlor · Srisawad) — the incumbents who will survive. So we cannot directly measure exit; we infer the opportunity surface: districts where AutoX demand / white-space exists but the big-4 are absent or thin, so the residual market is likely served by a smaller local operator (the exit-prone player). It answers "where could AutoX capture share if a marginal operator exits?" A true rival-fragility index needs a sub-scale-operator census — a blocked desktop / Thai-IP company-registry pull. Treat the score as ESTIMATED.

Out of line vs statistical twins · audit-first list ESTIMATED

Every branch is benchmarked against its 15 statistical twins — the branches with the most similar measured market fingerprint (10 km POI mix, district factories & workers, rainfall, own density, same NSO household-leverage backdrop) at least 25 km away. A branch whose risk proxy sits far above its twins is not just "in a risky market" — something local is different. Audit these first.

Most besieged branches · rivals on the doorstep MEASURED

Every branch is checked against the merged competitor census (Muangthai / Srisawad / Tidlor official store locators — measured-complete; Heng a sample). A branch is under siege when ≥3 rival branches sit within 2 km — these locations fight for the same walk-in borrower on the same street, so pricing and LTV pressure bite here first. Distances and counts are measured geometry; the ≥3 cutoff is a stated rule.
