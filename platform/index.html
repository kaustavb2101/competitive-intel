Rival fragility · where the competitor field thins under the Q1-2026 BoT deadline

Regulatory shake-out (Q1 2026): under the BoT responsible-lending framework (notification 25680030) auto title-loan operators need a personal-loan licence (≥฿50m capital, 28% interest cap). The registration window closes Q1 2026 — non-compliant, sub-scale / informal lenders risk forced exit. Tighter supervision favours scaled compliant incumbents (MTC, SAWAD, Tidlor, Heng — and AutoX) and squeezes small operators out, so the competitive field may thin as sub-scale rivals retreat. Source: docs/RESEARCH_DIGEST.md §A (Conf: High) · BoT 25680030.

Honest caveat — this is an ESTIMATED PROXY, not a measurement. The operators that will exit are exactly the sub-scale / informal lenders we do not census. Our competitor census covers only the four big, compliant brands (Heng · Muangthai · Tidlor · Srisawad) — the incumbents who will survive. So we cannot directly measure exit; we infer where the surviving rival field is thinnest: districts where local demand exists but the big-4 are absent or thin, so the residual market is likely held by a smaller local operator (the exit-prone player). It answers "where is the rival field most fragile under the regulatory shake-out?" A true rival-fragility index needs a sub-scale-operator census — a blocked desktop / Thai-IP company-registry pull. Treat the score as ESTIMATED.
